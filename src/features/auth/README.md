Signing in, and everything an account can do about itself: the three OAuth
providers, the state of paid access, the two links into the payment provider,
the export of what the server holds, the deletion of the account, and the sign
out that clears this browser. The warning shown before the browser leaves for
somebody else's domain lives here too, because leaving is what loses the buffer.

The payment button is written once and pressed from two places - the account
and the card on the pricing page - so that a person who has already paid is
offered the subscription they have rather than sold it again.
