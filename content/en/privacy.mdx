## 1. Who we are

RefScout checks academic manuscripts: it reads your document, examines the
references, the terms and the submission requirements, and gives you a list of
what a reviewer would notice. The data controller responsible for your personal
data is Insectivora Oy. "We", "us" and "our" refer to Insectivora Oy operating
RefScout. For any privacy question or request, contact
[support@refscout.app](mailto:support@refscout.app).

## 2. What happens to a document you bring

This is the part worth reading in full, because it is unusual and it is the
whole design of the product.

- **The file stays on your computer.** A PDF, a `.docx`, a `.tex`, a `.bib` or a
  `.md` is opened and read inside your browser, by code running on your own
  machine. The file itself is never uploaded, and there is no address on our
  servers that it goes to.
- **Only the extracted text is sent, and only when you press the button.**
  Adding a document to the buffer, reading it, correcting it and removing it all
  happen without any request leaving the page. When you run a check, the text of
  the documents you ticked is sent to our API, together with its length and a
  checksum, so that the findings can be pointed back at the right places.
- **Nothing about the file itself travels with it.** Not the file, not its
  contents in the format it was written in, and not the folder it came from —
  only text, the name you see on the card, and the settings of the checks you
  chose.

## 3. What stays in your browser

The text that was read out of your file is held **in the tab you are working
in**, and nowhere else. Closing the tab or reloading the page loses it, which is
why the screen says so and offers the download beside every result. Storage that
survives a reload is being built; this section will describe it when it exists,
and it will remain storage on your own device rather than on our servers.

Two small items are written to your browser's own storage and are not sent to us
at all:

- **`theme`** — whether you chose the light or the dark appearance, saved only
  when you use the switch.
- **Cloudflare security cookies (`__cf_bm`, `cf_clearance`)** — set by our CDN
  and security provider to tell humans from bots and protect the service.
  Strictly necessary for security.

Nothing here is used to track you, no advertising or cross-site cookie is set,
and no third-party script is loaded when you simply visit the site — which is
why there is no cookie banner to click away.

## 4. What we collect when a check runs

- **The text you submitted for checking**, for as long as the check is running.
- **The settings of that check** — which checks you asked for and the options
  you set on each document.
- **Server logs.** Standard web-server logs — IP address, timestamp, HTTP
  method, path, response code — for security and debugging.

Accounts, sign-in, saved sources and payment are not part of the product during
this phase: there is nothing to sign in to, and no billing data is collected.
When they arrive, this policy is updated before they do.

**Automatic error reports** are being built and are not being sent yet. When
they are, they will carry numbers, flags and error codes — how long something
took, how many pages a document had, which step failed — and never the text of
a document, a file name or anything you typed. The page will say so, and it will
be possible to turn off.

## 5. What we do not do

- We do not keep your manuscript after the check has finished.
- We do not use your content to train AI models, and the providers we use do not
  train on it under their API terms.
- We do not sell your personal data to anyone.
- We do not send any of your text anywhere except to run the check you asked
  for.

## 6. Why we process your data, and the legal basis

| Purpose                                                                 | Legal basis (GDPR Art. 6)           |
| ----------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------- |
| Run the check you asked for on the text you submitted                   | Performance of a contract — 6(1)(b) |
| Keep the service available, prevent abuse of it and of third-party APIs | Legitimate interests — 6(1)(f)      |
| Security logs                                                           | Legitimate interests — 6(1)(f)      |
| Error reports and product analytics, once they exist                    | Consent — 6(1)(a), opt-in           |

## 7. Who we share data with

We do not sell your data. We share it only with the providers needed to run a
check:

- **Anthropic (Claude API)** — the text of the passages a check has to judge is
  sent to Anthropic to produce the findings. Not used to train their models
  under the API terms.
- **Academic search providers** — Semantic Scholar, OpenAlex, Crossref, DBLP,
  Europe PMC, arXiv, DOAJ, HAL, IETF, CORE, bioRxiv/medRxiv and Unpaywall
  receive the titles, DOIs and search terms taken from your references in order
  to return matches. They do not receive your identity.
- **Cloudflare** — serves the site and protects it from abuse.

## 8. International transfers

Some providers process data in the United States. Where we transfer personal
data outside the EU and EEA, we rely on an appropriate safeguard:

- **Cloudflare and Google** — certified under the **EU-US Data Privacy
  Framework**.
- **Anthropic** — not certified under that framework; transfers are covered by
  the European Commission's **Standard Contractual Clauses** in Anthropic's
  data-processing terms.

These transfer mechanisms are re-verified periodically, as certifications can
change.

## 9. How long we keep data

- **The text sent for a check** — held while the check runs and deleted once its
  findings have been collected; it is not retained afterwards.
- **Server logs** — up to 30 days.
- **Backups** — purged on a rolling cycle of no more than six months.

These periods are our current defaults for the pilot and may be adjusted as the
service matures.

## 10. Your rights

If you are in the EU or EEA you have the right to:

- **Access** a copy of the personal data we hold about you;
- **Rectify** inaccurate data;
- **Erase** your data;
- **Restrict** or **object to** certain processing;
- **Data portability** — receive your data in a portable format;
- **Withdraw consent** at any time for anything based on consent. This does not
  affect processing done before withdrawal.

**How to exercise these rights.** Email
[support@refscout.app](mailto:support@refscout.app). Requests are handled
**manually** — we do not yet offer an automated self-service tool — and
completed within 30 days.

## 11. Right to complain

If you believe we have handled your data unlawfully, please contact us first so
we can try to resolve it. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the
Finnish supervisory authority, the **Office of the Data Protection Ombudsman**
(Tietosuojavaltuutetun toimisto):
[tietosuoja.fi](https://tietosuoja.fi/en/notification-to-the-data-protection-ombudsman).

## 12. Is providing data required?

To have a document checked, its text has to be sent — that is the service. There
is nothing else you are asked for: no account, no email address, and no
information about yourself.

## 13. Automated decision-making

RefScout's results are **academic writing aids**. They are suggestions and
checks to help you, not automated decisions that produce legal or similarly
significant effects about you. GDPR Article 22 does not apply. You remain
responsible for verifying results before relying on them.

## 14. Security

All traffic uses HTTPS. The parts of a check that need a server are reachable
only for the duration of a job and only with the token that job was created
with. The reading of your file happens in your browser, inside a worker with no
access to the network — which is also what makes the promise at the top of this
page enforceable rather than a matter of trust.

## 15. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the service changes. The "last updated" date at the
top reflects the most recent revision.
