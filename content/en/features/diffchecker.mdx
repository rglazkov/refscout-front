<Card title="What DiffChecker does">

- Side-by-side, line-by-line summary: removed, modified and added lines.
- Word-level highlighting — only the spans that actually changed are marked in a
  modified line, not the whole line.
- Pure paragraph reflow is detected and reported separately, so it does not
  masquerade as a real edit.
- Export the modified version as `.txt` or copy it.

</Card>

## Why this matters

"What actually changed between v3 and v4?" is hard to answer when a reformat
reflows every line. DiffChecker compares the two versions, highlights the exact
changed words, and separates content edits from pure whitespace reflow — so a
paragraph that only rewrapped does not drown out the one sentence you actually
rewrote.

It runs entirely in your browser: nothing is sent anywhere, and it costs no
quota.

## Examples

**Word-level change.** Old: `The model achieves 91% accuracy on the test set.`
New: `The model achieves 93.5% accuracy on the held-out test set.` DiffChecker
reports one modified line and highlights exactly `91 → 93.5` and the added
`held-out`.

## Questions people ask

**Does reflowed text show up as changes?** No. Pure paragraph reflow is detected
and reported separately.

**Is my document stored?** Nothing leaves your browser at all — there is nothing
to store.

**Does it need a Git history or a special format?** No. Bring the two versions
directly; no version control setup needed.

**Can it merge the two versions?** No. DiffChecker shows what changed; it does
not produce a merged file.
